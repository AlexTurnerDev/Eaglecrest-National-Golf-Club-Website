Learn Golf

The driver is how things get started on the course. Sometimes, it’s about how you start instead of how you finish. Because of how far a driver shot can go, it’s crucial to figure out the right form for you.

the iron club is another club in your bag. There are many different types of irons in your bag, and they each help with your desired distance and angle. From the fairway to the putting green, these irons can help you get to where you want to go.

next is the wedge. There is the pitching wedge, sand wedge, gap wedge, and a couple other variances. Each one has a specific purpose. They can help you get a high angle so that it lands on the green smoothly. Carefully, though. Don’t hit too hard or you could miss the green completely!

last but certainly not least is the putter. Probably the most important club in your bag. Unlike the other clubs, the putter doesn’t use a full swing. Rather, the club stays in your hands and below you the entire swing.
